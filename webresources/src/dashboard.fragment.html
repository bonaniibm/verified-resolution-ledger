Definitions
Reported deflection: Microsoft's bot deflection, deflected ÷ (deflected + escalated). A conversation the customer abandons still counts as deflected.
Verified containment: bot-only conversations where the same customer did not return about the same issue within the repeat window, over the same denominator.
Repeat-contact rate: decided contacts followed by a same-issue contact within the window, excluding scheduled follow-ups. Contacts with no reliable customer identity are excluded, because they can never be seen to repeat.
First-contact resolution: closed episodes resolved on their first and only contact. Derived from interactions, not surveys.
Cost per resolved outcome: AI credits + telephony + loaded labor of all closed episodes, over episodes that ended resolved.
Pending: contacts whose repeat window is still open are excluded from rates until they mature.
